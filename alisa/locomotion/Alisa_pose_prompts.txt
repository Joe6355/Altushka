Animation sequence left to right across top row, then left to right across bottom row:
1. CONTACT A: near/right leg extended forward RIGHT, front heel touches the floor, far/left leg trailing LEFT on its toe. Near arm BACK LEFT, far arm forward RIGHT.
2. DOWN A: near leg now planted under forward hip, knee flexes and weight lowers the body 1 pixel. The rear far foot peels off the ground and folds back, its heel rises. Arms approaching neutral.
3. PASS A: near leg is support straight under body, far leg swings forward with knee bent; far FOOT CLEARLY OFF FLOOR below and behind lifted knee. Hands near body. Body returns to middle height.
4. UP A: near leg supports behind body on toe; far thigh reaches FORWARD right, knee flexed and lower leg unfolding before landing. Body 1 pixel higher. Near arm begins swinging FORWARD.
5. CONTACT B: FAR/left leg now extended forward right, near/right leg extended backward left. Opposite of frame 1 in physical limb depth. Near arm FORWARD RIGHT, far arm BACK LEFT.
6. DOWN B: far leg takes weight forward with a slight bent knee, near foot lifts behind. Body 1 pixel lower.
7. PASS B: far leg supports under hips; NEAR leg bends and swings forward. Near foot CLEARLY LIFTED. Hands passing neutral.
8. UP B: far leg behind pushing with toe; near thigh extends forward right with bent knee and unfolding lower leg, preparing frame1.
